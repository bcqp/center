Governance

Responsibility for the series, and its independence.

Responsibility

The Founder and Executive Director is responsible for the series, including the topics chosen, the data used and the briefs published. He is accountable for corrections.

Independence

BIQEP is an independent research initiative. It is not a government body and is not affiliated with Bangladesh Bank, the Bangladesh Bureau of Statistics or the Government of Bangladesh.
