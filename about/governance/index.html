Governance

Who decides what is published, and how decisions can be challenged.

Responsibility

The Founder and Executive Director is responsible for the series. Nothing is published without his review and approval, and he is accountable for corrections.

Decision path

01

Topic chosen

The author sets the priority topic. Press reports can suggest it but cannot decide it.

02

Data validated

Each series is checked against its original release. The result is recorded in the validation log.

03

Draft prepared

Automated tools may help to collect data, run models and draft text. They cannot publish.

04

Author review

The author reads the draft, checks the figures and decides whether to publish.

05

Correction

If an error is found later, it is corrected in public under the corrections policy.

Independence

BIQEP is an independent research initiative. It is not a government body and is not affiliated with Bangladesh Bank, the Bangladesh Bureau of Statistics or the Government of Bangladesh.
