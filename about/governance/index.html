Governance

Responsibility for the series, and its independence.

Institutional responsibility

BIQEP's Founder and Executive Director is responsible for the institute's strategic direction and oversight of its research activities. Responsibility for individual outputs, editorial decisions, data preparation and technical review should be identified in the relevant publication or project record. Leadership information is presented separately on the Leadership page.

Research independence

BIQEP is an independent research initiative. Research questions, analytical methods and conclusions are governed by scholarly considerations and disclosed evidence. The institute does not claim to be a government agency, statutory regulator, university department or representative of Bangladesh Bank, the Bangladesh Bureau of Statistics, the Government of Bangladesh or any international organisation.

Decision-making and accountability

  • Research outputs should identify responsible authors and material contributors.
  • Data and methodological choices should be documented for informed scrutiny.
  • Conflicts of interest and relevant funding or support should be disclosed.
  • Corrections should be assessed and recorded transparently.
  • External collaboration does not confer control over BIQEP's independent conclusions unless a formal agreement explicitly defines otherwise.

Publication and editorial responsibility

BIQEP policy briefs and institute research outputs are distinct from any proposed scholarly journal initiative. The institute's outputs are governed by relevant BIQEP policies. The proposed Journal of Quantitative Economics and Public Policy remains a separate initiative in development; editorial appointments, submission arrangements and publication procedures will be announced only when formally established.

Data stewardship

Data sources, retrieval dates, transformations and validation decisions should be recorded. BIQEP will use publicly accessible official data where possible and respect third-party access conditions and licensing. Confidential or restricted data should not be disclosed through public research materials.

Corrections and complaints

Factual or methodological concerns should identify the affected publication, claim, figure or dataset and provide supporting evidence where available. Substantive errors are assessed under the Corrections Policy. Publication standards, source documentation and conflicts of interest are addressed in the institutional policies.

Limits of this governance statement

This page describes BIQEP's current governance principles and responsibility framework. It is not evidence of a legally incorporated entity, independent governing board, external accreditation, formal institutional partnership or statutory recognition. Any such status will be stated only when supported by formal documentation.
