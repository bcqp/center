About BCQP

An independent research initiative advancing quantitative economics, econometrics, statistical sciences and evidence-based public policy.

A center for quantitative research and public policy

The Bangladesh Center for Quantitative Policy (BCQP) is an independent research initiative focused on the application of economic theory, mathematical methods, statistics and econometrics to questions of economic performance and public policy. Its work is designed to connect rigorous empirical analysis with practical questions facing Bangladesh and the wider international research community.

BCQP develops research outputs, data resources, methodological materials and computational tools. The center's public-policy work is one part of this broader programme; the institutional agenda also includes quantitative economics, statistical sciences, research training and open research infrastructure.

A quantitative result is useful when its data, assumptions, method and limitations can be examined.

Research independence

BCQP is an independent research initiative. It is not a government body and does not claim affiliation with Bangladesh Bank, the Bangladesh Bureau of Statistics, the Government of Bangladesh or the international institutions whose public datasets and methodological resources it cites. References to external sources identify data or methodological resources; they do not imply endorsement, partnership or certification.

Research outputs and standards

BCQP's outputs include policy briefs, research notes, data documentation, statistical reports and replication materials. Each empirical output should identify its data sources, transformations, methods, assumptions and limitations. The center's policies describe its approach to publication, validation, replication, corrections and conflicts of interest.
