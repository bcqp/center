Leadership

BIQEP is co-founded by Dr. Tuhin G. M. Al Mamun and Dr. Abdur Rahim.

Dr. Tuhin G. M. Al Mamun

Co-Founder · First Founder

Dr. Tuhin G. M. Al Mamun is an economist and quantitative researcher and Co-Founder of the Bangladesh Institute of Quantitative Economics and Public Policy (BIQEP). He holds a PhD in Economics from Hannam University, South Korea, where his doctoral research examined monetary policy transmission, regional economic heterogeneity, and macroeconomic dynamics.

His research focuses on structural macroeconomic modelling, monetary and fiscal policy, Bayesian econometrics, time-series analysis, and computational economics. His methodological expertise includes Bayesian vector autoregression, dynamic stochastic general equilibrium (DSGE) models, heterogeneous-agent New Keynesian (HANK) frameworks, Markov-switching models, and machine-learning applications in economic research.

His broader research interests include climate and energy economics, distributional consequences of economic policy, financial stability, and evidence-based public policy. He is also the developer of Open Econometric Systems (OES), an open computational platform designed to make advanced econometric methods more accessible to researchers.

At BIQEP, he contributes to the institute's research strategy, quantitative methodology, policy analysis, and development of reproducible, data-driven research.

Dr. Abdur Rahim

Co-Founder

Dr. Abdur Rahim is an economist and researcher in the Department of Economics at Hannam University, South Korea. His research focuses on macroeconomics, behavioral economics, labor economics, and econometrics, with an emphasis on applying quantitative methods to contemporary economic and public policy challenges.

His research contributions span several areas of applied economics, including public sector efficiency, energy economics, environmental sustainability, and macroeconomic modelling. His methodological interests include applied econometric analysis, quantitative modelling, and empirical investigation of economic behavior, institutional performance, and policy outcomes.

His interdisciplinary research connects macroeconomic dynamics with labor-market outcomes, behavioral responses, energy transitions, and public sector performance. Through his research, he seeks to contribute to evidence-based economic analysis and a deeper understanding of policy challenges.

As Co-Founder of the Bangladesh Institute of Quantitative Economics and Public Policy (BIQEP), he contributes to the institute's academic development, collaborative research activities, and advancement of quantitative economic research for public policy.
