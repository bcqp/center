Dr. Tuhin G. M. Al Mamun

Founder and Executive Director

Dr. Al Mamun holds a PhD in Economics. His research covers monetary policy transmission, heterogeneous-agent and structural macroeconomic models, Bayesian and time-series econometrics, and applied machine-learning methods.

He is responsible for the topics chosen, the data validated, the models run and every brief published in the series.
