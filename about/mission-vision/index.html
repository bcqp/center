About

Mission and vision

What the series is for, and the standard it holds itself to.

Mission

To make quantitative evidence on Bangladesh’s economy easier to find, check and use. The series publishes short analysis built on official data, estimated with open methods, and stated with its limits.

Vision

A public conversation about economic policy in which every number has a visible source, every model can be rerun, and uncertainty is stated rather than hidden.

Commitments

  • Official sources. Evidence comes from Bangladesh Bank, the Bangladesh Bureau of Statistics, the IMF and the World Bank.
  • Open methods. The estimation engine is open source, and each brief states the settings needed to repeat its results.
  • Clear limits. Each brief states its data limits and what its results cannot show.
  • Public corrections. Errors are corrected on the page, with the date and what changed.
