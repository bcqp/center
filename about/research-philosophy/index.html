Research philosophy

How we think about evidence, models and uncertainty.

Measurement first

A model is only as good as its data. Before anything is estimated, each series is traced to its original release, its definition is read, and breaks in it are noted. In Bangladesh’s consumer price index, for example, the base year changed in April 2023, and any analysis across that date has to say how it handled the change.

Methods fit the question

Vector autoregressions and local projections suit questions about dynamics and timing. Structural and Bayesian models suit questions that need explicit assumptions. Panel and threshold methods suit questions about differences across groups or regimes. The method is chosen for the question, and the reason is stated.

Say what the design cannot show

Most macro-econometric evidence shows association and timing, not proof of cause. Each brief states which claims its design supports and which it does not.

Robustness is part of the result

A finding that disappears when the sample, lag length or specification changes is reported as fragile. Alternative specifications are published with the main one.

Replicable by design

The estimation engine is open, the data are versioned with a retrieval date, and the settings are listed, so a reader can repeat the work.
