Data notes

A data note sets out verified figures and open questions for a topic. It makes no model claims.

Purpose and scope

A BIQEP data note is a concise, source-led record of a statistical issue relevant to Bangladesh's economy or public policy. It identifies the observed value or pattern, explains how the indicator is defined and records what can and cannot be inferred from it. A data note is not a forecast, causal evaluation or econometric result unless a separate analytical design is explicitly presented.

Minimum evidence record

  • Indicator: exact name and statistical concept.
  • Producer: institution responsible for the source release.
  • Dataset and table: direct source link, table identifier or publication reference.
  • Observation: value, unit, frequency, geographic coverage and reference period.
  • Release information: publication date, retrieval date and vintage where available.
  • Definition: denominator, population, classification, base year and relevant methodology.
  • Limitations: revisions, breaks, missing observations, estimation or provisional status.

Primary statistical sources

For Bangladesh, data notes should prioritise the relevant primary producer: Bangladesh Bureau of Statistics for official national and social statistics; Bangladesh Bank economic data for monetary, financial and external-sector series; and the Finance Division for fiscal and budget information. The IMF's Bangladesh national-accounts metadata documents aspects of compilation and source practices and can help readers understand statistical context.

How BIQEP assesses a reported figure

The note should verify that the selected observation matches the source's definition, unit, period and release vintage. Where a secondary international database is used, the note should identify it as a harmonised or disseminated source and, where possible, trace the observation to the national producer. The UN National Quality Assurance Framework provides a general reference for statistical quality dimensions, while the SDMX standards initiative supports structured exchange and documentation of statistical data and metadata.

Interpretation: facts versus questions

Each note separates (i) the reported observation, (ii) a comparison over time or across groups, (iii) plausible mechanisms that require further analysis and (iv) open questions. A correlation or simultaneous movement is not described as a causal effect. Where an indicator is revised, rebased or reclassified, the note explains whether historical values remain comparable.

Revision and citation policy

When the source agency revises a series, BIQEP should record the new retrieval date and update the note if the change affects its interpretation. Citations should point to the original release or table, not only to a general institutional homepage. Each note should preserve enough source detail for a reader to locate the same observation.
