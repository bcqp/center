BIQEP policy research repository

The Policy Briefs section brings together concise policy analysis and supporting research records produced under the Bangladesh Institute of Quantitative Economics and Public Policy (BIQEP). The repository is organised so readers can distinguish a policy interpretation from the underlying data record, a recurring macroeconomic overview, a statistical compilation and the materials needed to reproduce an empirical result.

BIQEP's policy analysis focuses on Bangladesh's macroeconomic performance, monetary and fiscal policy, labor markets, external-sector adjustment, finance, climate and energy, growth and human development. The research agenda is connected to the institute's research programme, Data Hub and methodological framework.

Five record types

Data notes

Focused evidence notes that identify a statistical observation, its source, definition, reference period and relevant caveats. They describe what the data show without presenting an unsupported causal or model claim. Explore data notes.

Bangladesh Economic Outlook

Periodic synthesis of selected macroeconomic and financial indicators. Each issue should state the data cut-off, latest available observations, changes in definitions and the distinction between observed outcomes and forward-looking scenarios. Explore the outlook.

Statistical reports

Structured tables and analytical summaries derived from identified official releases. Reports document source tables, units, transformations, coverage and retrieval dates. Explore statistical reports.

Replication materials

Code, data references, model specifications and computational settings that support independent reproduction of published results, subject to data licences and access restrictions. Explore replication materials.

Policy briefs

Interpretation of evidence for a defined policy question. A brief should distinguish descriptive facts, econometric estimates, causal findings and policy recommendations, and state the limitations of each.

Evidence and citation practice

Claims about official statistics should cite the original producing agency and the specific release, table or dataset wherever possible. For Bangladesh, source discovery includes the Bangladesh Bureau of Statistics, Bangladesh Bank economic data and the Finance Division, Ministry of Finance. The IMF Dissemination Standards Bulletin Board provides metadata and information on national statistical practices; it is a documentation resource, not a substitute for the primary release.

International comparisons may draw on the World Bank World Development Indicators, IMF Data, ILO ILOSTAT and UN SDG indicator metadata. Comparability must be assessed indicator by indicator; common labels do not guarantee common definitions or coverage.

How to read a BIQEP output

  • Source: identify the statistical agency, dataset, table or publication.
  • Reference period: distinguish the observation period from the publication or retrieval date.
  • Measurement: check units, base year, population coverage, seasonal adjustment and classification.
  • Method: identify whether the result is descriptive, predictive, causal or model-based.
  • Uncertainty: review confidence or credible intervals, sensitivity analysis and known data limitations.
  • Reproducibility: consult the associated replication materials when they are available and legally shareable.

Publication status and corrections

Only records displayed in the repository are published records. A collection page or planned series does not imply that a report has already been released. Corrections and material revisions should be documented on the affected record and handled under the institute's Corrections Policy. See the institutional policies for publication and research standards.
