Why is inflation still above 8%?

A data note. It sets out the official figures verified so far and the question still to be tested: why inflation has stayed high even as food prices are said to fall and the policy rate is said to be high. Both premises are still to be verified. No model results yet.

Tuhin G. M. Al Mamun, BIQEPNovember 2026Draft, not yet approved for publication

What we can say so far

  • Point-to-point CPI inflation was 8.34% in September 2026, against 8.26% in August and 8.36% in September 2025. Over a year it has moved by only 0.02 percentage points.
  • The 12-month average fell from 9.45% to 8.65% over the same year. The 12-month average is falling while the point-to-point rate is not.
  • The policy rate and the food and non-food split are not yet verified, so this note does not use them.

What the official data show

Headline inflation is measured by the consumer price index (base 2021-22 = 100). The two measures below give different pictures. The point-to-point rate compares one month with the same month a year earlier. The 12-month average smooths over the year.

The question

Headline inflation has stayed a little above 8% for a year. Two explanations are often given in public discussion: that food prices are no longer the driver, and that a high policy rate should be pulling inflation down. This brief will test what the data support. Nothing here is a finding yet.

Still to verify

  • Policy rate. Press reports and a Bangladesh Bank policy statement give different levels (10.00% and 9.50%). The level will be taken from the original Bangladesh Bank announcement before use.
  • Food and non-food inflation. To be read from the BBS monthly CPI release, not from news summaries.
  • Wage rate index and exchange rate. To be taken from BBS and Bangladesh Bank releases.
  • Change of CPI base. From April 2023 the index uses base 2021-22. The estimation sample must treat that break explicitly.

Planned analysis

Using monthly food and non-food inflation, wages, the exchange rate and the policy rate, the brief will estimate a vector autoregression and local projections in Open Econometric Systems. It will report the specification, sample and diagnostics, and say what the design cannot show. Results, charts and replication files will be added here after validation and review.
