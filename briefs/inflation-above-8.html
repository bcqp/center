Why is inflation still above 8%?

Tuhin G. M. Al Mamun, BIQEP. This is a data note, not a finished analysis. The model results are still to come.

What the official data show

Headline inflation, measured by the consumer price index (base 2021-22), is slightly above 8% and has barely changed over a year, while the 12-month average has fallen.

MeasureSep 2026Aug 2026Sep 2025
Inflation, point to point8.34%8.26%8.36%
Inflation, 12-month average8.65%8.66%9.45%

Source: Bureau of Statistics (BBS), as published on the Bangladesh Bank economic data page, retrieved 14 Nov 2026.

Still to verify

The current policy rate is not yet confirmed: press reports and a Bangladesh Bank policy statement disagree (10% against 9.50%). It will be checked against the original Bangladesh Bank announcement before use.
