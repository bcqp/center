Contact

Research collaboration, data corrections and policy questions.

For current enquiries, use the public BIQEP website repository issue tracker. Please do not post confidential, personal or unpublished sensitive information in a public issue. A dedicated institutional contact address will be added when one is formally established.

Corrections are welcome

If a figure does not match its official source, tell us which brief, which figure and which release it conflicts with. We will check it against the original and publish a dated correction if you are right. See the corrections policy.

Collaboration and questions

We welcome conversation on research collaboration, methods and the data used in the briefs. Please name the brief or series you are writing about. See research collaboration.

What to include

  • the relevant BIQEP page, brief or research area;
  • a concise description of the question or proposed contribution;
  • for a correction, the figure or claim and the official release used for comparison;
  • for collaboration, the research question, proposed methods, data and expected contribution.

Enquiry routes

  • Research collaboration: use the collaboration framework to structure a proposal.
  • Data and methodological questions: consult the Data hub and Methodology pages first.
  • Website corrections: report a broken link or factual website issue through the repository issue tracker.
