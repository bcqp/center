Series register

Every series used in a brief, with its status, official source and retrieval date. A series is marked verified only after it has been checked against the original release.

How to read this page. “Verified” means the value was read from the official release on the date shown. “Pending” means it has not been checked, so no value is shown. We do not fill gaps with estimates or press reports.

Break in the CPI series

From April 2023 the BBS consumer price index uses base year 2021-22. Any estimate that spans that date must treat the change of base explicitly. Each brief says how it did so.
