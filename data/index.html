Series register

Every series used in a brief, with its official source and retrieval date.

How to use the series register

The register is BIQEP's inventory of statistical series used in its research and policy outputs. It is intended to make each observation traceable to a named publisher, an identifiable indicator and a stated reference period. A row should be read as a record of a particular series and source, not as a general endorsement of every dataset published by that institution.

Data provenance and comparability

The register records, where available, the source publisher, indicator name, unit, reference period, frequency, retrieval date, status and known transformation or break. The original national statistical or administrative source is preferred when it provides the relevant series and documentation. International databases are used for comparative analysis or where their harmonised series are appropriate to the research question. The Bangladesh Bank economic data portal, Bangladesh Bureau of Statistics and Finance Division data portal are key starting points for Bangladesh macroeconomic, price, labor and fiscal information.

Cross-country data are not automatically comparable. Differences in definitions, collection methods, coverage, fiscal years, revisions, currency conversion and missing-data treatment can affect interpretation. The World Bank's data compilation methodology describes consistency and aggregation procedures; its data quality guidance discusses recognised quality frameworks. For international comparisons, inspect dataset-level metadata in IMF Data, World Development Indicators and ILOSTAT.

Interpretation of register status

  • Verified: the displayed observation has been checked against the stated source and reference period; this is not a general certification of the source's entire statistical system.
  • Official source: identifies the publisher, not an endorsement of BIQEP's analysis or interpretation.
  • Derived: transformed, aggregated, deflated, seasonally adjusted or otherwise calculated values must be labelled and distinguished from directly published observations.
  • Pending: the source match or required metadata has not yet been established sufficiently for a verified status.
  • Revised: the source has changed a historical observation or its methodology; the retrieval date and data vintage are therefore material.

Transformations and derived indicators

Every transformation should be reproducible from the stated inputs. Growth rates must identify the comparison period; real series must identify the deflator and base year; ratios must define numerator and denominator; currency conversions must state the exchange-rate convention. Interpolated or imputed observations must be flagged and must not be presented as directly observed official figures. Where a series is rebased, the linking or splicing procedure and its assumptions should be documented.

Structural breaks and the CPI base change

The BBS consumer price index has used base year 2021–22 from April 2023. Analysis spanning the change must identify the relevant CPI series and explain how observations across the base transition are treated. The BBS remains the primary source for its CPI releases; see BBS and the IMF Dissemination Standards Bulletin Board for statistical metadata and dissemination context. A change in base or methodology should not be treated as an ordinary month-to-month movement.

Register limitations

The presence of a series in this register does not imply that it is complete, final, free of measurement error or suitable for every research purpose. Source releases may be revised, discontinued or reclassified. Users should consult the cited source's latest notes before reusing an observation. Where the register does not provide a release identifier or retrieval date, the record should be treated as requiring further documentation rather than assumed to be fully audited.
