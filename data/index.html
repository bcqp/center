Data hub

Every series used in a brief, with its status, official source and retrieval date. A series is marked verified only after it has been checked against the original release.

How to read this page. “Verified” means the value was read from the official release on the date shown. “Pending” means it has not been checked yet, so no value is shown. We do not fill gaps with estimates or press reports.

Official sources we use

Bangladesh Bank

Economic data, policy rates, exchange rates, monetary statistics.

Bangladesh Bureau of Statistics

Consumer prices, wage rate index, national accounts, labor force survey.

IMF

International Financial Statistics, World Economic Outlook, country reports.

World Bank

World Development Indicators and Bangladesh country data.

Break in the CPI series

From April 2023 the BBS consumer price index uses base year 2021-22. Any estimate that spans that date has to treat the change of base explicitly. Each brief states how it did so.
