Official sources

The publishers whose releases we treat as evidence, and what we take from each.

Primary national statistical sources

Source selection is indicator-specific. BIQEP aims to cite the institution that produces the statistic and the exact release, table or dataset used. A portal that republishes a series is identified as the dissemination channel, not substituted for the original producer when that distinction is known.

Bangladesh Bank

Use for monetary aggregates, interest rates, exchange rates, balance of payments, reserves, remittances and banking statistics. Start with the Economic Data portal and the National Summary Data Page. Confirm the relevant table, unit, frequency and release vintage before citing an observation.

Bangladesh Bureau of Statistics

Use for consumer prices, wage indices, national accounts, labor-force and household or social statistics. Consult the BBS official portal for the original publication and accompanying methodology. Survey estimates should be read with their sampling, weighting, coverage and reference-period documentation.

Finance Division, Ministry of Finance

Use for budget documents, fiscal tables, revenue and expenditure information and public-finance publications. Begin at the Finance Division data portal. Distinguish budget estimates, revised estimates and actual execution, and record the fiscal year and institutional coverage.

National Board of Revenue

For tax and customs administration statistics, consult the NBR official portal and the specific release or table. Tax collection totals may differ across sources because of coverage, accounting period, classification or later revisions; document the exact concept used.

International statistical reference systems

IMF Data

Macroeconomic, financial, balance-of-payments and government-finance datasets. Use dataset-specific metadata and release notes.

IMF Dissemination Standards Bulletin Board

Country metadata and information on statistical dissemination practices and standards. It is a documentation resource, not an independent validation of every BIQEP observation.

World Development Indicators

Cross-country development indicators with source and methods documentation.

ILOSTAT

Labor-market statistics and metadata on concepts, definitions and classifications.

BIS Statistics

International banking, credit, debt securities, exchange-rate and financial statistics.

UN SDG Metadata

Indicator-level definitions, disaggregation and custodian-agency documentation.

OECD Data

Internationally comparable economic, social, environmental and policy indicators.

How a source is cited in BIQEP outputs

  1. Name the original producer and the specific dataset, table, release or report.
  2. Provide a direct link to the release or dataset rather than only an institutional homepage.
  3. Record the observation period separately from publication and retrieval dates.
  4. State the unit, frequency, geographic coverage, base year and definition relevant to the statistic.
  5. If a secondary portal republishes the statistic, identify both the original producer and the dissemination source when this is known.
  6. Where sources differ, explain the difference in coverage, definition, vintage or method rather than selecting a value without explanation.

Metadata and quality references

The UN National Quality Assurance Framework provides a general reference for statistical quality assurance. The SDMX initiative provides standards for the exchange and documentation of statistical data and metadata. These are reference frameworks; their inclusion does not imply formal certification or endorsement of BIQEP.

Source limitations

Official status does not eliminate revisions, sampling error, non-sampling error, definitional changes or reporting lags. International datasets may harmonise or transform national inputs. Users should consult the metadata for the exact series and cite the release vintage used in the analysis.
