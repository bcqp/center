Official sources

The publishers whose releases we treat as evidence, and what we take from each.

Bangladesh Bank

Economic data, policy announcements, exchange rates, interest rates, monetary and bank statistics.

Bangladesh Bureau of Statistics

Consumer price index, wage rate index, national accounts and the Labour Force Survey.

International Monetary Fund

International Financial Statistics, World Economic Outlook and country reports.

World Bank

World Development Indicators and Bangladesh country data.

How a source is cited

  • The original publisher is named, not the site that relayed it.
  • The address of the release page and the retrieval date are recorded.
  • If Bangladesh Bank republishes a BBS figure, we say so, as in “BBS, published by Bangladesh Bank”.
  • Where sources differ, the original release is followed.
