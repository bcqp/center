Our standard

Verified before published.

01

Official sources first

News is a lead, never evidence. Numbers come from Bangladesh Bank, the Bangladesh Bureau of Statistics, the IMF and the World Bank.

02

Open methods

Estimates run in the open-source Open Econometric Systems engine, so results can be replicated.

03

Honest about uncertainty

Each brief states its data limits, its assumptions and what the results cannot show.

04

Reviewed by the author

Drafts are prepared with automated tools. Nothing is published until the author has reviewed and approved it.
