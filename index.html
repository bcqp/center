Quantitative economics · Econometrics · Public policy

Understanding economic and social policy through quantitative evidence.

BCQP is an independent research center advancing quantitative economics, econometrics, statistical sciences and evidence-informed public policy. We develop research, analyse data and evaluate policy questions in Bangladesh and internationally.

Founded by Dr. Tuhin G. M. Al Mamun and Dr. Abdur Rahim.

Analytical approach

Methods follow the research question.

BCQP uses time-series and panel econometrics, causal evaluation, statistical learning and structural modelling according to the data, estimand and mechanism under study.
