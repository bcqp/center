Quantitative economics · Econometrics · Public policy

Understanding economic and social policy through quantitative evidence.

BIQEP is an independent research institute advancing quantitative economics, econometrics, statistical sciences and evidence-informed public policy. We develop research, analyse data and evaluate policy questions in Bangladesh and internationally.

Founded by Dr. Tuhin G. M. Al Mamun and Dr. Abdur Rahim.

Inflation, the Taka and the stance of policy in Bangladesh, 1992–2025

Inflation rose to 10.5% in 2024 while lending rates stayed below it in each of the four years from 2022 to 2025. Nine econometric estimates, using 13 official macro variables, examine exchange-rate pass-through to inflation.

Money growth and prices in Bangladesh

Peer benchmarks and five model specifications covering 12 variables from 1992–2025 examine money, credit, interest rates, prices and growth, with comparisons across regional economies.

Analytical approach

Methods follow the research question.

BIQEP uses time-series and panel econometrics, causal evaluation, statistical learning and structural modelling according to the data, estimand and mechanism under study.
