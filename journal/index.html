Journal of Quantitative Economics and Public Policy

A proposed scholarly journal initiative of BIQEP, being developed for future submissions from researchers worldwide across quantitative economics, econometrics, statistics and public policy.

About the journal

Quantitative research for economic and public-policy questions.

Under the working title Journal of Quantitative Economics and Public Policy (JQEP), the planned publication will be an open-submission scholarly journal. When submissions open, researchers, academics and practitioners from any institution or country will be able to submit original manuscripts within the journal's scope. Authors will not need to be affiliated with BIQEP.

Submissions will be evaluated through an independent editorial process and peer review under published editorial policies. The journal will operate separately from BIQEP's institute-led policy research and policy-brief series: BIQEP produces its own policy analysis, while JQEP provides an open scholarly venue for external and internal authors alike.

Development status

The journal is in development and submissions are not yet open. The title is provisional and remains subject to final title-availability checks and the ISSN registration process. No ISSN has been assigned. The journal intends to accept manuscripts from researchers worldwide, without requiring BIQEP affiliation. Submission dates, editorial appointments, publication schedule, fees and indexing information will be announced only after they are formally established.

Editorial principles

Academic standards before publication.

The journal is being designed as an open-submission publication with independent editorial judgement, transparent peer review, clear research-integrity policies and accessible publication information.

  • Submissions open to researchers worldwide, regardless of institutional affiliation
  • Independent editorial decisions and handling editors
  • Peer review by qualified subject specialists
  • Clear authorship, conflict-of-interest and correction policies
  • Research ethics and publication-integrity procedures
  • Transparent information on fees, licensing and archiving before submissions open
Part of BIQEP

Research, methods and publication.

BIQEP's policy research and policy briefs will remain institute-led outputs. JQEP will be a separate scholarly publication that invites original submissions from the wider research community. Both will share BIQEP's broader commitment to quantitative methods and transparent evidence.

Proposed editorial framework

How the journal is intended to operate.

The proposed editorial model separates manuscript assessment from the institute's policy-research activities. A submission would first undergo an editorial scope and completeness check. Manuscripts considered suitable would then be assigned to an editor and evaluated by independent subject specialists. Editorial decisions would be based on scholarly quality, methodological appropriateness, originality, clarity and relevance to the journal's scope—not on author affiliation, nationality or institutional status.

01

Submission and initial screening

Check scope, completeness, originality declarations, author information, data and ethics statements, and compliance with submission requirements.

02

Editorial assessment

An editor assesses the manuscript's relevance, contribution, methodological coherence and suitability for external review. A manuscript may be declined at this stage where it falls outside scope or does not meet the journal's threshold.

03

Independent peer review

Suitable manuscripts are intended to be evaluated by independent reviewers with relevant expertise. The review model, reviewer guidance and confidentiality arrangements will be published before submissions open.

04

Editorial decision and revision

The handling editor considers reviewer reports and the authors' responses. Decisions and revision requirements should be communicated with reasons and recorded in the manuscript history.

05

Production and publication

Accepted manuscripts undergo copyediting, author proofing, metadata preparation and final publication checks. Publication dates, identifiers, licensing and archiving details will be stated for each article.

This describes the proposed workflow. It is not a statement that the journal is currently accepting manuscripts or has completed its editorial appointments.

Potential contributions

Article types under consideration

Original research articles

Substantive theoretical, empirical or methodological contributions supported by a clearly specified research design and evidence.

Review articles

Critical and systematic synthesis of a defined research literature, with transparent search, selection and analytical procedures where applicable.

Methods and software papers

New or carefully evaluated quantitative methods, statistical algorithms, computational workflows and research software, with sufficient technical documentation.

Replication and research notes

Reproducibility studies, robustness assessments, concise empirical findings and technically focused research notes.

Policy evaluation studies

Quantitative evaluations of economic and public policies, with explicit estimands, identification strategies, assumptions and limitations.

Data and measurement papers

Documented datasets, measurement strategies, indicator construction and statistical resources, subject to rights and data-access conditions.

Final article categories, manuscript limits and formatting requirements will be specified in the author instructions before the journal opens.

Research integrity

Standards for responsible scholarly publishing.

The journal's final policies are intended to address authorship and contributorship, originality, plagiarism screening, duplicate submission, conflicts of interest, funding disclosure, research involving human participants, data availability, image and figure integrity, corrections, expressions of concern and retractions.

Policy development will draw on the public guidance of the Committee on Publication Ethics (COPE), the ICMJE Recommendations where relevant to submitted work, and the World Association of Medical Editors as a source of broader editorial guidance. These are reference resources; their mention does not imply membership, certification or endorsement.

Research transparency

  • Authors should describe data sources, sample construction, variables, methods and material limitations.
  • Empirical claims should be supported by sufficient methodological detail to permit critical assessment.
  • Data, code and supplementary materials should be shared where legally and ethically permissible, with restrictions explained.
  • Funding, relevant interests and the role of sponsors should be disclosed.
  • Corrections to the scholarly record should be handled through a documented process.

For empirical economics, the American Economic Association's data and code policy provides a useful external reference for documentation and verification expectations.

Open access and discoverability

Publication information will be disclosed before launch.

The journal's access model, copyright and licensing terms, author charges (if any), waiver arrangements, long-term preservation arrangements and publication schedule have not yet been finalised. These details will be made public before submissions open. No author should infer that publication is free, that an article-processing charge applies, or that a particular licence has been selected until the journal publishes its formal terms.

Potential directories and infrastructure relevant to scholarly publishing include Directory of Open Access Journals (DOAJ), Crossref and the ISSN International Centre portal. Listing, membership, DOI registration, ISSN assignment and indexing are separate processes with their own eligibility requirements. BIQEP does not currently claim that JQEP has an ISSN, Crossref membership, DOAJ listing or indexing in Scopus or Web of Science.
