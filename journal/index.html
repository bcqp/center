Journal of Quantitative Economics and Public Policy

A planned peer-reviewed scholarly journal of the Bangladesh Institute of Quantitative Economics and Public Policy (BIQEP), dedicated to rigorous quantitative research and evidence-informed public policy.

About the journal

Quantitative research for economic and public-policy questions.

The proposed Journal of Quantitative Economics and Public Policy (JQEP) will provide an academic forum for original research that advances economic analysis, statistical methodology and the evaluation of public policy.

The journal is being developed as part of BIQEP's broader research and scholarly-publishing programme. Its editorial structure, publication schedule, peer-review procedures and author guidelines will be announced after they are formally established.

Development status

This journal is in the planning and development stage. Submissions are not currently open. No publication fees, issue dates, indexing status or editorial appointments are being announced at this stage.

Proposed scope

Research areas

Quantitative economics and econometrics

Time-series, panel-data, Bayesian, structural and computational econometric methods.

Applied statistics and data science

Statistical theory and applications, machine learning, statistical computing and reproducible analysis.

Macroeconomics and financial economics

Monetary and fiscal policy, macroeconomic dynamics, financial stability and structural modelling.

Energy, climate and environmental economics

Energy transitions, climate policy, environmental outcomes and distributional effects.

Development economics and public policy

Policy evaluation, causal inference, labor markets, inequality and institutional performance.

Computational and reproducible research

Open methods, transparent workflows, replication materials and computational tools for research.

Editorial principles

Academic standards before publication.

The journal is intended to operate with independent editorial judgement, transparent peer review, clear research-integrity policies and accessible publication information.

  • Independent editorial decisions
  • Peer review by qualified subject specialists
  • Clear authorship, conflict-of-interest and correction policies
  • Research ethics and publication-integrity procedures
  • Transparent information on fees, licensing and archiving before submissions open
Part of BIQEP

Research, methods and publication.

The proposed journal will complement BIQEP's policy research, quantitative methodology and open research infrastructure.
