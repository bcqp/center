Methodology

Data standards

How series are recorded, versioned and described.

Every series carries

  • its official name and publisher;
  • the address of the release page it was read from;
  • the period covered and the unit;
  • the date it was retrieved;
  • its status: verified or pending.

Breaks and revisions

  • CPI base. From April 2023 the BBS consumer price index uses base year 2021-22. Analysis across that date states how the change was handled.
  • Revisions. Official figures can be revised. A brief states the retrieval date of its data, and figures are re-checked before publication.
  • Seasonality. A brief states whether series are seasonally adjusted. We do not adjust a series without saying so.
  • Transformations. Logs, differences and growth rates are listed with the specification.

What is left out

A series that cannot be traced to an official release, or whose definition cannot be established, is not used.
