Methodology

Data standards

How series are recorded, versioned and described.

Minimum metadata for every series

A data series is usable only when its statistical meaning and provenance can be identified. BIQEP records the original indicator name, producing institution, dataset or table, direct release address, unit, frequency, geographic and population coverage, reference period, publication date and retrieval date. Where available, the source vintage and methodological notes are retained.

Definitions, units and coverage

  • Concept: use the producer's official definition and distinguish similarly named indicators.
  • Unit: record currency, scale, percentage, index, physical unit or rate denominator.
  • Coverage: identify population, institutions, geography and sectoral scope.
  • Frequency: distinguish monthly, quarterly, annual and irregular observations.
  • Time reference: distinguish calendar year, fiscal year, survey reference period and release date.
  • Price basis: identify current or constant prices, deflator, index base and rebasing method.

Primary sources and source hierarchy

For Bangladesh, use the original producer wherever possible: Bangladesh Bureau of Statistics for national accounts, prices, labor and social statistics; Bangladesh Bank for monetary, financial and external-sector series; and the Finance Division for fiscal and budget information. The IMF Dissemination Standards Bulletin Board provides country metadata and dissemination information that help users understand definitions and compilation practices.

Revisions, breaks and vintages

Official statistics may be revised as new information becomes available or methods change. Each analysis should record the vintage used and avoid silently mixing observations from different releases. Structural breaks may arise from rebasing, classification changes, survey redesign, changes in institutional coverage or new compilation methods. The treatment of a break—separate samples, linking, splicing or explicit break modelling—must be stated and justified.

Seasonal adjustment and transformations

State whether a series is seasonally adjusted by the producer or by the analyst. If adjusted by the analyst, document the method and settings. Logs, differences, growth rates, ratios, deflation, currency conversion, aggregation and standardisation should be defined mathematically or procedurally. Derived series must remain distinguishable from source observations.

Bangladesh CPI base transition

The BBS CPI series has used base year 2021–22 from April 2023. Analysis that spans the transition should identify the series used and explain how comparability across the change is handled. Consult the BBS releases and relevant IMF country metadata rather than assuming a mechanical splice is valid for every purpose.

International standards and metadata frameworks

The Statistical Data and Metadata eXchange (SDMX) provides standards for structuring and exchanging statistical data and metadata. The UN National Quality Assurance Framework provides a general framework for statistical quality management. The UN SDG metadata repository demonstrates indicator-level documentation of definitions, methods and custodianship. These are reference frameworks, not certifications of BIQEP data.

When a series is excluded

A series is not used for a quantitative claim when its definition cannot be established, its source cannot be traced sufficiently, its units or reference period cannot be reconciled, or a material break remains unexplained. Where a provisional or incomplete series is used for a limited purpose, its status and limitations are stated explicitly.
