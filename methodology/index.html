Methodology

How each brief is made

1. Topic

A priority topic is chosen each week. News and market signals only suggest a question.

2. Official data

Series come from Bangladesh Bank, BBS, the IMF and the World Bank. Retrieval dates are recorded.

3. Validation

Every figure is checked against its original source. When sources disagree, we resolve it or leave the figure out.

4. Estimation

Models run in Open Econometric Systems (OES), an open-source engine. Specifications and code are published with each brief.

5. Review

A draft is drafted with automated tools, then reviewed and approved by the author before publication.
