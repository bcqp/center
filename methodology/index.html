How a brief is made

From official data to estimates: how series are sourced, checked and modelled.

01

Official data

Series come from Bangladesh Bank, the Bangladesh Bureau of Statistics, the IMF and the World Bank. The source, address and retrieval date of each series are recorded in the Data hub.

02

Validation

Each figure is checked against its original release before it is used.

03

Estimation

Models run in Open Econometric Systems (OES), an open-source engine. The specification, sample and settings are published with the brief.

04

Limits and corrections

Each brief states its data limits and what its results cannot show. If a figure is found not to match its official source, it is corrected and the correction is noted on the page with its date.

Analytical design: match the method to the estimand

BIQEP distinguishes descriptive measurement, prediction and causal or structural analysis. These answer different questions and are not interchangeable. The analytical note for each output should state the target quantity, sample, unit of observation, time dimension, identifying variation and assumptions required for interpretation.

Time-series analysis

Depending on integration properties and the research question, methods may include dynamic regression, ARDL and error-correction models, VAR/VECM, local projections, state-space models and structural time-series approaches. Stability, residual dependence, lag choice and parameter constancy are assessed as relevant.

Panel-data analysis

Panel specifications consider unit and time effects, dynamic adjustment, slope heterogeneity, cross-sectional dependence, common shocks and the structure of missing observations. Pooled restrictions are not imposed without an analytical rationale.

Causal and programme evaluation

Difference-in-differences, event studies, instrumental variables, regression discontinuity and related designs are considered only when the treatment assignment mechanism and identifying assumptions can be stated and assessed.

Prediction and machine learning

Predictive performance is assessed with validation designs that respect the data-generating structure, including temporal ordering where appropriate. Predictive accuracy is not presented as evidence of a causal effect.

Bayesian and structural modelling

Bayesian models document likelihoods, priors, posterior computation and convergence diagnostics. Structural models state the economic mechanisms, equilibrium conditions, calibration or estimation strategy and counterfactual assumptions.

Robustness and uncertainty

Robustness checks are selected to probe plausible threats to the result, not to search mechanically for significance. Estimates are reported with uncertainty measures and economically meaningful magnitudes.

International statistical and documentation frameworks

  • IMF statistical manuals and guidance provide internationally recognised concepts and compilation guidance for macroeconomic statistics.
  • The Government Finance Statistics Manual provides a framework for fiscal statistics and public-sector analysis.
  • The UN SDG metadata repository documents indicator definitions, methodologies and custodianship for global SDG indicators.
  • The Statistical Data and Metadata eXchange (SDMX) initiative provides standards for exchanging statistical data and metadata.

These resources inform definitions and documentation; they do not certify an individual BIQEP estimate. Each output remains responsible for its own data provenance, model specification, diagnostics and interpretation.

What every brief states

  • the data used, with source and retrieval date;
  • the sample period and any break in the series, such as the 2023 change of CPI base;
  • the model, the identifying assumptions and the main robustness checks;
  • what the results cannot show, including causal claims the design does not support.
