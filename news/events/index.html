Seminars and talks.

Public events and research exchange

BIQEP uses this page to announce confirmed seminars, research talks, workshops, training sessions and public discussions related to quantitative economics, econometrics, statistical sciences and public policy. Event listings are intended to provide clear practical information and a reliable record of activities open to researchers, students, practitioners and interested members of the public.

Event formats

Research seminars

Presentation and discussion of working papers, empirical findings, theoretical developments and policy-relevant research.

Methods workshops

Practical sessions on econometric methods, statistical computing, data management, reproducibility and quantitative research design.

Public policy discussions

Evidence-led discussion of macroeconomic developments, public finance, labor markets, climate and energy transitions and human development.

Training and short courses

Structured learning activities in statistics, econometrics, quantitative policy analysis and research tools, with prerequisites and intended audiences stated in each notice.

Event information standard

Each confirmed listing should provide the event title, date and time with time zone, venue or online format, language, organiser, speakers and affiliations where confirmed, intended audience, registration instructions and accessibility or participation details. Where an event has limited capacity or requires registration, the notice should state the applicable arrangements.

Participation and announcements

Invitations, calls for papers and registration details are published only when the relevant arrangements are confirmed. A proposed event is not presented as scheduled, and an invited speaker or prospective partner is not described as confirmed until participation has been agreed. Changes or cancellations should be posted promptly and the original notice updated with the relevant date.
