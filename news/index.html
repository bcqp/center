BIQEP institutional website expanded

The institute website is being developed to present BIQEP's broader research remit across quantitative economics, econometrics, statistical sciences, public policy, data resources and research training. The site also documents data sources, validation practices, methodology and institutional policies.

This entry records a website-content update; it does not announce a new institutional partnership, accreditation or external endorsement.

Research infrastructure

Open research and reproducibility

BIQEP's online resources connect research outputs with data documentation, methodological notes and replication materials. Open Econometric Systems (OES) is founder-developed research infrastructure initiated by BIQEP's Founder and Executive Director, Dr. Tuhin G. M. Al Mamun. It is presented as a computational resource supporting applied econometric work.

What BIQEP reports here

This page is the institute's dated public record for verifiable organisational and research activity. News items may cover publication releases, new datasets or research tools, methodological resources, institutional announcements, collaborations that have been formally confirmed and significant website updates. Each item should state what happened, when it happened and which public record or source supports the announcement.

News publication standards

  • Use a clear publication date and distinguish the date of an event from the date an announcement is posted.
  • Identify the responsible person, unit or organisation where relevant and authorised.
  • Link directly to the publication, dataset, event page or primary announcement supporting the item.
  • Describe collaborations, funding, affiliations and endorsements only when formally confirmed; do not imply them from informal discussions.
  • Distinguish completed activities from planned activities, proposals and expressions of interest.
  • Correct material inaccuracies transparently under the Corrections Policy.

Research and evidence updates

When a news item reports a quantitative finding, readers should be able to follow it to the relevant publication, source data and methods. BIQEP's Data Hub records series provenance; the Validation Log describes source-checking procedures; and the Methodology section explains analytical approaches. External statistical references include the IMF Dissemination Standards Bulletin Board and the UN National Quality Assurance Framework, which provide metadata and quality-assurance context rather than endorsement of BIQEP.

Events and public engagement

Seminars, workshops, lectures and research discussions are listed separately on the Events page. An event is listed as confirmed only when its date, format and relevant participation details have been established. Proposed or forthcoming activities are labelled accordingly.

News entries are dated and limited to verifiable institute activities. Corrections and changes to published research are recorded under the relevant publication and correction policies.
