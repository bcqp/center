Use of automated tools

What automated tools may and may not do.

The policy

  • Automated tools may help to collect data, run models and draft text.
  • They do not decide what is true: every figure is validated against its official source.
  • They cannot publish. The author reviews and approves each brief.
  • The use of automated tools is stated on the site, in the About page and the methodology.
