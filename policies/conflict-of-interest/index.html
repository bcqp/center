Conflict-of-interest policy

Disclosure and independence.

Purpose

This policy supports the independence and credibility of BCQP's research and policy publications by requiring relevant interests to be disclosed and assessed. A conflict of interest is a circumstance that could reasonably be perceived to influence, or appear to influence, an author's, contributor's or decision-maker's judgement.

Types of interests

  • Financial: employment, consulting fees, grants, honoraria, investments or other financial relationships relevant to the subject.
  • Professional: advisory roles, paid expert work, leadership positions or professional commitments.
  • Institutional: affiliations or organisational responsibilities connected to the subject or entities discussed.
  • Personal: relationships or circumstances that could reasonably be perceived as relevant to the analysis.

Disclosure requirements

Authors and contributors should disclose relevant interests when a publication is prepared. Disclosures should identify the nature of the interest, the relevant organisation or relationship and the period to which it relates. A statement that no relevant interests exist should be included where applicable. Funding and other material support should be disclosed separately where relevant.

Assessment and management

BCQP assesses disclosures in context. Possible responses include a public disclosure, independent review of a specific section, recusal from a publication decision, reassignment of responsibilities or declining work where a conflict cannot be managed adequately. The response should be proportionate to the nature and significance of the interest.

Editorial independence and commissioned work

BCQP's analytical conclusions should not be altered to satisfy a funder, sponsor, data provider or interested party. Any commissioned or supported work should disclose the nature of the relationship and the role, if any, of the supporting party in research design, data access, analysis, drafting and publication decisions. BCQP does not accept paid or sponsored briefs under its current stated operating policy.

Institutional independence

References to Bangladesh Bank, the Bangladesh Bureau of Statistics, the Government of Bangladesh or international organisations identify data producers or sources where relevant. Such references do not imply affiliation, partnership, endorsement or approval of BCQP's analysis.

External reference

The Committee on Publication Ethics (COPE) provides general resources on competing interests and publication ethics. This policy is BCQP's internal procedure and does not imply COPE membership or endorsement.
