Copyright and reuse

Who owns what, and how to reuse it.

Purpose and scope

This policy explains how BIQEP identifies ownership, attribution and permitted reuse of materials appearing in its publications and digital resources. Rights may differ between BIQEP-authored text and graphics, third-party datasets, official statistics, photographs, maps, software and other incorporated content.

Third-party data and materials

Official statistics remain subject to the rights, licences and terms established by their publishers. Users must consult the relevant source terms before copying, redistributing or adapting datasets, tables, charts or other materials. Citing a source is necessary for attribution but does not by itself grant permission to reuse protected material.

BIQEP-authored content

Unless a publication states a different licence or rights arrangement, BIQEP-authored text, charts and tables should be attributed to the Bangladesh Institute of Quantitative Economics and Public Policy (BIQEP), with the publication title, date and URL. Do not imply that BIQEP endorses a reuse, adaptation or third-party product.

Attribution and adaptation

  • Identify the original author, publisher or data producer.
  • Give the publication or dataset title, version or reference period and a direct source link where available.
  • State whether a figure or table has been modified, recalculated, translated or adapted.
  • Preserve relevant licence notices and comply with attribution or share-alike requirements.
  • Do not remove source notes or present third-party data as independently collected by BIQEP.

Software and code

Software and code are governed by their own licences. Users should inspect the licence distributed with the relevant software or repository before copying, modifying or redistributing code. This policy does not replace the terms of an individual software licence.

External reference points

The Creative Commons licence descriptions explain common permissions and conditions for licensed content. The World Intellectual Property Organization copyright resources provide general information on copyright. Applicable law and the specific rights holder's terms govern any particular reuse.

Permission requests

Where permission is required, users should contact the relevant rights holder. For BIQEP-authored material, contact BIQEP through the Contact page and identify the material, proposed use, format and distribution context.
