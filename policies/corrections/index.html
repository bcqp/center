Corrections policy

What happens when something is wrong.

Purpose

This policy governs corrections to BIQEP material after publication. The aim is to preserve a reliable public record, make material changes visible and explain whether a correction affects the interpretation or conclusions.

How to report an error

Reports should identify the publication, page or section, affected statement, figure or table, the suspected problem and supporting source or evidence. Submit the report through the Contact page. BIQEP may request clarification or supporting documentation before assessing the report.

Assessment and correction

  1. Identify the affected record and the precise claim or element.
  2. Check the original source, calculation, code or editorial record relevant to the issue.
  3. Assess whether the issue is typographical, factual, statistical, methodological or interpretive.
  4. Determine whether the correction changes a value, method, interpretation or conclusion.
  5. Publish a dated correction note describing what changed and when.

Materiality and presentation

Minor typographical changes that do not affect meaning may be corrected without a separate notice where appropriate. Changes to data, calculations, methods, interpretations or conclusions should be clearly disclosed. If a correction changes the main conclusion, that fact should be stated prominently on the affected publication. Earlier versions should remain identifiable where technically feasible.

Retraction or withdrawal

Where a publication cannot be relied upon because of a serious error, unavailable or unauthorised data, or another substantial problem, BIQEP may withdraw or mark the record as retracted. The public notice should state the reason and date without silently removing the publication history.

External reference

The Committee on Publication Ethics (COPE) provides guidance on corrections, retractions and publication-record integrity. BIQEP's procedure is an internal policy and does not imply COPE membership or endorsement.
