Data and sources policy

What counts as evidence.

Purpose and source hierarchy

This policy governs the selection, attribution and documentation of data used in BCQP research and publications. The preferred source is the original statistical producer or responsible administrative institution. A secondary database may be used when appropriate, but the publication should identify it as the dissemination source and, where known, name the original producer.

Source selection

  • Use official national statistical or administrative sources for Bangladesh when they provide the relevant measure and documentation.
  • Use international databases for harmonised comparisons or where their coverage and definitions suit the research question.
  • Prefer a specific release, table or dataset link over a general institutional homepage.
  • Assess whether definitions, units, population coverage, frequency and reference periods match the intended analysis.
  • Do not treat an institution's official status as proof that every series is complete, final or suitable for every purpose.

Bangladesh and international sources

Relevant Bangladesh sources include Bangladesh Bureau of Statistics, Bangladesh Bank and the Finance Division. International comparative sources include IMF Data, World Development Indicators, ILOSTAT and UN SDG metadata. Dataset-specific metadata must be consulted before use.

Required source documentation

Each series should record, where available: publisher; dataset, table or release; direct URL; indicator definition; unit; frequency; geographic and population coverage; reference period; publication and retrieval dates; data vintage; licence or access terms; and relevant methodological notes.

Transformations and derived data

Analyst-created values must be distinguishable from directly published observations. Growth rates, ratios, deflated series, currency conversions, seasonal adjustments, interpolations and imputations should be documented with the inputs and calculation method. The SDMX standards and UN National Quality Assurance Framework provide external references for structured metadata and statistical quality documentation.

Licensing and access

Data are used subject to the source publisher's licence, terms of use and applicable access restrictions. Public availability does not automatically grant permission to redistribute a dataset. Restricted or confidential data should not be published in a way that breaches access conditions.
