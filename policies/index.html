The standards each brief is held to: publication, sources, validation, replication, corrections and the use of automated tools.

Policies

How this series is run.

These policies state the standards every brief is held to. They describe how the series works, not legal terms.

01

Publication policy

A brief is published only after the data are validated and the author has reviewed and approved the text. Work that has not met that standard is marked “in preparation” and makes no model claims.

02

Source and data policy

Figures come from Bangladesh Bank, the Bangladesh Bureau of Statistics, the IMF and the World Bank. News reports may suggest a question but are never a source. Each series is listed in the Data hub with its source and retrieval date.

03

Validation policy

Every figure is checked against the original release. Where sources disagree, the original announcement decides. If it cannot be settled, the figure is left out rather than guessed.

04

Replication policy

Models are run in the open-source Open Econometric Systems engine. Each brief states the data, specification and settings needed to repeat the analysis.

05

Corrections policy

A figure that does not match its official source is corrected, and the correction is noted on the page with its date. Report one through the contact page.

06

Use of automated tools

Automated tools help to collect data and draft text. They do not publish anything. The author reviews and approves every brief.

07

Independence

BIQEP is an independent research initiative. It is not a government body and is not affiliated with Bangladesh Bank, the Bangladesh Bureau of Statistics or the Government of Bangladesh. Briefs describe evidence and are not investment, legal or policy-implementation advice.
