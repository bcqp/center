Series policies

The standards each brief is held to: publication, sources, validation, replication, corrections, reuse and independence.

Purpose and scope

This Policy Framework brings together the principles and operating rules that guide BIQEP's research, evidence preparation and publication. It is an index to the institute's specific policies; each linked policy governs its own subject and should be read for the detailed requirements.

Core principles

Traceable evidence

Quantitative claims should be linked to identifiable sources, definitions, reference periods and release vintages. Statistical quality principles are informed by the UN National Quality Assurance Framework.

Methodological transparency

Publications should state the research question, analytical method, assumptions, limitations and uncertainty. Descriptive, predictive and causal conclusions must be distinguished.

Reproducibility

Where data access and licensing permit, code, specifications and computational settings should support independent checking. The American Economic Association data and code policy is an external reference for empirical documentation.

Accountability

Material errors, relevant interests, attribution and reuse conditions should be handled transparently under the relevant policy below.

How the policies work together

The policies form a connected publication process. Data and Sources governs what evidence is selected and how it is attributed; Validation governs how observations are checked; Publication governs how analysis is presented and approved; Replication governs supporting materials; Copyright and Reuse governs lawful use of materials; Conflict of Interest addresses independence and disclosure; and Corrections governs changes after publication.

External reference frameworks

BIQEP's procedures are informed by relevant external documentation, including the UN National Quality Assurance Framework, SDMX standards, the AEA data and code policy and the FAIR principles. These are reference materials only; their mention does not imply that BIQEP is accredited, certified, affiliated with or endorsed by those organisations.

Application and review

These policies apply to BIQEP materials published through the institute's website unless a specific publication states otherwise. The framework and individual policies may be updated as the institute's activities and publication procedures develop. Each page should be read with its current revision date and any publication-specific disclosure.
