Publication policy

When a record may be published.

The policy

  • A brief is published only after its data have been validated and the author has reviewed and approved the text.
  • Work that has not met that standard is marked “in preparation” and makes no model claims.
  • Records are kept in separate collections by kind: briefs, data notes, outlook, statistical reports and replication materials.
  • A published record shows its date, its author and its data retrieval date.
