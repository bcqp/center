Publication policy

What a published record contains.

Purpose and editorial scope

This policy governs the preparation and publication of BIQEP policy briefs, data notes, economic outlooks, statistical reports and replication records. The purpose is to ensure that published material states what question it addresses, what evidence it uses and what conclusions its methods can support.

Publication requirements

  • Each record identifies its title, publication date, author or responsible contributors and record type.
  • It states the research or policy question, data sources, reference period, method and material limitations.
  • Quantitative claims are linked to the relevant source, table, dataset or release wherever available.
  • Empirical results distinguish descriptive patterns, predictive performance, causal estimates and model-based scenarios.
  • Uncertainty, sensitivity and relevant data limitations are reported in proportion to the claim.
  • Any funding, relevant interests, third-party material and data-access restrictions are disclosed.

Analytical and editorial assessment

Before publication, a record should be checked for source traceability, internal consistency, methodological coherence, clarity of interpretation and appropriate attribution. The depth of assessment is proportionate to the type and complexity of the output. This process is an internal BIQEP quality procedure and should not be described as external peer review unless independent reviewers have in fact been engaged.

Authorship and responsibility

Named authors are responsible for the work attributed to them, including the accuracy of descriptions of data and methods. Contributions should be acknowledged appropriately. Institutional publication does not imply that an external data provider or referenced organisation endorses BIQEP's analysis.

Corrections and publication record

Published records remain identifiable after material changes. Corrections should be dated and explained under the Corrections Policy. Data provenance is governed by the Data and Sources Policy, while the Validation Policy describes observation checks.

Relevant external references

The American Economic Association data and code policy provides an external reference for empirical transparency. The Committee on Publication Ethics (COPE) provides broader publication-ethics resources. These references inform good practice but do not imply BIQEP membership, accreditation or endorsement.
