Validation policy

How figures are checked.

Purpose and scope

This policy describes how BIQEP checks statistical observations used in its publications. A validation result applies to the specific indicator, source, period, unit and data vintage checked. It is not a blanket certification of a dataset, institution, statistical system or analytical conclusion.

Minimum validation procedure

  1. Define the observation: identify the indicator, unit, frequency, coverage and reference period.
  2. Locate the source: retrieve the original release, table or dataset and record its URL and dates.
  3. Reproduce the value: compare the observation using consistent units, precision and period conventions.
  4. Inspect metadata: review revisions, rebasing, classification changes, footnotes and breaks.
  5. Record the outcome: document the result, vintage, retrieval date and any discrepancy or limitation.

Validation outcomes

  • Verified: the recorded value matches the cited source for the stated period and unit under the documented comparison rule.
  • Pending: source, metadata or observation match is not yet sufficiently established.
  • Revised: a later source vintage changes the observation or historical series.
  • Not comparable: definitions, units, coverage or reference periods differ materially.
  • Derived: the value is calculated from source inputs and must be checked against its documented formula and inputs.

Revisions and structural breaks

Preliminary figures may be revised; indices may be rebased; survey methods and classifications may change. BIQEP records the vintage used and explains material changes affecting interpretation. A value that matches a source release can still be unsuitable for a particular research question or estimator.

Documentation and external references

Checks should be recorded in the Validation Log with the source, indicator, period, unit, retrieval date, result and note. The UN National Quality Assurance Framework and World Bank data-quality guidance provide external reference points. BIQEP's checks are internal procedures and are not a formal assessment under those frameworks.

Limits of validation

Source matching does not establish causal validity, model correctness, absence of measurement error or fitness for every estimand. Those questions require separate methodological assessment. See the Data and Sources Policy and Methodology.
