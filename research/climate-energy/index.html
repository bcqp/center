Research area

Climate and energy

Energy prices, green finance and climate risk.

Why it matters

Energy prices feed into costs, inflation and the budget, and climate risk adds a longer-run channel. The questions here link macro data to energy and climate sources.

Key questions

  • How do energy-price changes pass into consumer prices?
  • What are the fiscal costs of energy price policy?
  • How might climate-related shocks affect growth and prices?
  • How large is green finance relative to the credit system?

Data sources

  • Official energy price notifications
  • Bangladesh Bureau of Statistics
  • World Bank: climate and development data

Series used in briefs are listed, with their sources, in the Data hub.

Methods that fit

  • Structural VARs
  • Local projections
  • Panel threshold models

Models run in Open Econometric Systems. The method for each brief is chosen for its question and stated in the brief.
