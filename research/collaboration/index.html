Research collaboration

How to work with the series on data, methods and questions.

Purpose of collaboration

BIQEP welcomes research exchange with academics, economists, statisticians, data scientists, policy researchers and practitioners. Collaboration is intended to produce transparent, methodologically defensible work on economic and public-policy questions, with clear responsibilities and documented contributions.

Potential forms of cooperation

Joint research

Co-develop research questions, empirical strategies, manuscripts and policy analyses. Proposed projects should identify the research question, expected contribution, data requirements and intended output.

Methods and replication

Exchange code, estimation strategies, replication files and methodological reviews. Contributions should document software versions, model specifications and computational requirements.

Data and measurement

Discuss official data sources, measurement choices, revisions, comparability and reproducible data construction. Restricted or confidential data should not be shared through public channels.

Research seminars and training

Explore seminars, guest lectures, technical workshops and collaborative training in econometrics, statistics and quantitative policy analysis. Activities are subject to agreement and scheduling.

How to propose a collaboration

  1. State the research problem and why it matters.
  2. Identify the proposed contribution and relevant literature.
  3. Describe the data, methods and likely limitations.
  4. Specify the proposed roles, authorship expectations and deliverables.
  5. Indicate a realistic timeline and any funding or resource requirements.

Research integrity and independence

Collaboration does not guarantee publication or endorsement by BIQEP. Authorship should reflect substantive scholarly contributions and be agreed before manuscript submission. Funding, competing interests, data restrictions and institutional affiliations should be disclosed. Findings must remain subject to transparent analysis and appropriate review.

What BIQEP cannot promise

Contact or proposal submission does not create a formal partnership, institutional affiliation, appointment, funding commitment or guaranteed publication. Any formal arrangement must be agreed explicitly by the parties and documented in writing.
