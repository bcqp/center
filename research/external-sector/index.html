Research area

External sector

Exchange rate, remittances, reserves and trade.

Why it matters

Bangladesh’s exchange rate, remittances and reserves link the domestic economy to the world. Changes there pass into prices, the budget and bank balance sheets.

Key questions

  • How do remittances respond to the exchange rate and to policy?
  • What do reserves tell us about external resilience?
  • How do exchange-rate moves pass into import and consumer prices?
  • What happens to trade flows around large depreciations?

Data sources

  • Bangladesh Bank: exchange rate, balance of payments and remittance data
  • IMF: International Financial Statistics
  • World Bank: Bangladesh data

Series used in briefs are listed, with their sources, in the Data hub.

Methods that fit

  • Nonlinear ARDL
  • Vector autoregressions
  • Structural-break tests
  • Wavelet coherence

Models run in Open Econometric Systems. The method for each brief is chosen for its question and stated in the brief.
