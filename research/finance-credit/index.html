Research area

Finance and credit

Bank lending, interest rates and financial stability.

Why it matters

Credit and bank health decide how much of a policy change reaches firms and households. Stability questions need bank-level and system-level data read together.

Questions we plan to ask

  • How does credit growth respond to interest rates and liquidity?
  • Where does bank risk concentrate, and how does it move over time?
  • How do deposit and lending rates move relative to the policy rate?
  • What threshold effects appear in credit and stability indicators?

Planned official sources

  • Bangladesh Bank: scheduled bank statistics and interest rate data
  • Bangladesh Bank: financial stability reports

Sources are listed as planned. A series is used only after it has been checked against its original release, and is then recorded in the Data hub.

Methods that fit

  • Panel methods
  • Threshold regression
  • Quantile regression

Models run in Open Econometric Systems. The method for each brief is chosen for its question and stated in the brief.
