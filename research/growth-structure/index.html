Research area

Growth and structure

Investment, savings, sector shares and urbanisation.

Why it matters

Growth depends on how much is invested and where. Comparing Bangladesh with regional peers shows where the gaps are.

Key questions

  • How much does investment lift output?
  • Is the economy shifting toward higher-productivity sectors?
  • How does Bangladesh compare with peers on savings and investment?

Data sources

  • World Bank: World Development Indicators
  • Bangladesh Bureau of Statistics: national accounts

Series used in briefs are listed, with their sources, in the Data hub.

Methods that fit

  • Bayesian VARs
  • Local projections
  • Peer benchmarking

Models run in Open Econometric Systems. The method for each brief is chosen for its question and stated in the brief.
