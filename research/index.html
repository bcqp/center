Research agenda

Seven areas of work. They describe the questions we intend to study, not claims about briefs already published.

The agenda

Each area page lists the questions we plan to ask, the official sources we plan to use and the methods that suit them. An item moves from the agenda to a brief only when its data have been validated.

From agenda to brief

Across all areas

  • Data come from official releases, with source and retrieval date recorded.
  • Models run in the open-source Open Econometric Systems engine, so results can be replicated.
