Research area

Inflation

Price dynamics, food and non-food inflation, and expectations.

Why it matters

Inflation touches every household budget. Understanding what drives it, how fast shocks pass through, and why it can stay high is the starting point for most policy questions in Bangladesh.

Key questions

  • How much of headline inflation comes from food prices, and how much from non-food prices?
  • How quickly and how fully do exchange-rate movements pass into consumer prices?
  • How do wage growth and inflation affect each other?
  • Does the 2023 change in the CPI base year change what we can say about trends?

Data sources

  • Bangladesh Bureau of Statistics: consumer price index, food and non-food
  • Bangladesh Bank: economic data pages

Series used in briefs are listed, with their sources, in the Data hub.

Methods that fit

  • Vector autoregressions and local projections
  • ARDL bounds tests
  • Markov-switching models for regimes

Models run in Open Econometric Systems. The method for each brief is chosen for its question and stated in the brief.
