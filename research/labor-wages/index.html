Research area

Labor and wages

Employment, real wages and the informal economy.

Why it matters

Whether wages keep pace with prices decides how inflation is felt. Labor data in Bangladesh come from surveys with their own definitions, which has to be handled with care.

Questions we plan to ask

  • Are nominal wages keeping pace with consumer prices?
  • How do real wages differ across sectors?
  • How large is informal employment, and how does it respond to shocks?
  • How does the labor market respond to monetary and fiscal changes?

Planned official sources

  • Bangladesh Bureau of Statistics: wage rate index
  • Bangladesh Bureau of Statistics: Labour Force Survey

Sources are listed as planned. A series is used only after it has been checked against its original release, and is then recorded in the Data hub.

Methods that fit

  • Panel methods
  • Quantile regression
  • ARDL models

Models run in Open Econometric Systems. The method for each brief is chosen for its question and stated in the brief.
