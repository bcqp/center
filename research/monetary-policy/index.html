Research area

Monetary policy

Policy rates, transmission to lending and the exchange rate.

Why it matters

Bangladesh Bank’s policy decisions work through banks, credit and the exchange rate before they reach prices and output. The lags and strength of that chain are an empirical question.

Key questions

  • How do policy-rate changes pass through to deposit and lending rates?
  • What are the lags from the policy rate to inflation and output?
  • How does the exchange rate respond to policy changes?
  • How has transmission changed across policy regimes?

Data sources

  • Bangladesh Bank: policy announcements and monetary policy statements
  • Bangladesh Bank: interest rate and monetary statistics

Series used in briefs are listed, with their sources, in the Data hub.

Methods that fit

  • Structural VARs
  • Bayesian VARs
  • Time-varying-parameter VARs
  • Local projections

Models run in Open Econometric Systems. The method for each brief is chosen for its question and stated in the brief.
