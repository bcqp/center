Research area

Public finance

Revenue, spending, deficits and debt sustainability.

Why it matters

How the government raises and spends money shapes growth, inflation and the room for policy. Fiscal questions need careful sources, because definitions and coverage differ across documents.

Key questions

  • How do revenue and spending respond to growth and prices?
  • What does the debt path look like under stated assumptions?
  • What are the size and timing of fiscal multipliers?
  • How do energy and food subsidies affect the budget?

Data sources

  • Finance Division, Ministry of Finance: budget documents
  • National Board of Revenue: revenue data
  • IMF country reports

Series used in briefs are listed, with their sources, in the Data hub.

Methods that fit

  • Structural VARs
  • Local projections
  • Threshold models
  • Bayesian VARs

Models run in Open Econometric Systems. The method for each brief is chosen for its question and stated in the brief.
