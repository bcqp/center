Biography

Dr. Tuhin G. M. Al Mamun is an economist and quantitative researcher and Co-Founder of the Bangladesh Center for Quantitative Policy (BCQP). He holds a PhD in Economics from Hannam University, South Korea, where his doctoral research examined monetary policy transmission, regional economic heterogeneity, and macroeconomic dynamics.

His research focuses on structural macroeconomic modelling, monetary and fiscal policy, Bayesian econometrics, time-series analysis, and computational economics. His methodological expertise includes Bayesian vector autoregression, dynamic stochastic general equilibrium (DSGE) models, heterogeneous-agent New Keynesian (HANK) frameworks, Markov-switching models, and machine-learning applications in economic research.

His broader research interests include climate and energy economics, distributional consequences of economic policy, financial stability, and evidence-based public policy. He is also the developer of Open Econometric Systems (OES), an open computational platform designed to make advanced econometric methods more accessible to researchers.

At BCQP, he contributes to the center's research strategy, quantitative methodology, policy analysis, and development of reproducible, data-driven research.
