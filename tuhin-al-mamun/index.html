Dr. Tuhin G. M. Al Mamun

Tuhin Al Mamun is a Bangladeshi economist and the founder of BIQEP Policy Briefs. His work is on monetary policy transmission, econometrics and macroeconomic modeling.

About Tuhin Al Mamun

Dr. Tuhin G. M. Al Mamun (also written Tuhin Al Mamun) is an economist from Bangladesh. He holds a PhD in Economics, with a dissertation on monetary policy transmission in Bangladesh.

He is the founder and executive director of the Bangladesh Institute of Quantitative Economics and Public Policy (BIQEP) and the author of BIQEP Policy Briefs, a series of data-based briefs on the Bangladesh economy that use official statistics and reproducible methods.

Research interests

  • Monetary, fiscal and environmental policy
  • Dynamic stochastic general equilibrium and heterogeneous-agent macroeconomic models
  • Bayesian estimation, time-series and regime-switching econometrics
  • Applied machine learning for economics: double machine learning, LASSO, random forests and gradient boosting

Open Econometric Systems

Tuhin Al Mamun develops Open Econometric Systems (OES), an open library of 69 econometric models that the BIQEP briefs use for their estimates. See the OES page and the source code on GitHub.
